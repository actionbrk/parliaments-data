# Data licence

The files in `data/` are published under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with one exception: seat counts.

## Seat counts: the source's terms

Each chamber file names, in its `source` field, where its seat counts come from. The counts stay under that source's own licence or terms of use, recorded with the source in `data/sources.yaml` (`license`, `license_url`). Reusing them means following those terms, including the credit a source prescribes, which is its `name`.

## Everything else: CC BY 4.0

CC BY 4.0 covers the rest of `data/`, to the extent it is ours to license:

- which parties each parliamentary group is made of;
- the list of parties and their links to Party Facts;
- the links to English Wikipedia articles on parties, groups, chambers and parliaments;
- the colours given to groups;
- the identifiers of chambers, groups, parties and sources.

To credit it, name "parliaments-data contributors" and link to this repository, alongside the credit each seat-count source requires.
